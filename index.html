About Ayyapa Wood Works

Crafted With Experience

Ayyapa Wood Works specializes in custom woodwork and handcrafted wooden doors. The portfolio focuses on door designs that can be tailored by style, wood type, carving detail and finish.

We serve homes in Proddatur, Kadapa and the nearby villages and towns around them. Pick a design you like, share your measurements, and we build it to your size, wood and finish.

Our Services

Wooden Doors & Woodwork in Proddatur and Kadapa

Ayyapa Wood Works makes custom wooden doors and woodwork to order for Proddatur, Kadapa and the nearby villages and towns around them — in your size, wood, finish and carving details.

Main Doors

Custom teak main entrance doors for homes and villas, in traditional and modern designs.

Pooja Doors

Carved wooden pooja room doors with Ganesh, Lakshmi and Venkateswara carvings and sacred symbols.

Double Doors

Wide double doors with carved panels, decorated frames and brass jaali work.

Carved Doors

Deep hand-carved doors with lattice panels, floral designs and medallions.

CNC Carving

Precision CNC-carved panels and patterns, finished by hand in our workshop.

Windows

Wooden windows and louvered door-and-window sets with carved frames.

Pooja Mandirs

Custom wooden pooja mandirs with temple-style tops, carved pillars and brass bells.

Furniture & Interiors

Bespoke wooden furniture — cots, cupboards, TV units, dining tables, chairs, benches, staircases with carved railings and jaali partitions.

Choukattu (Door & Window Frames)

Strong wooden choukattu frames for doors and windows, made to your measurements.

Wood Polishing (Thopda)

Wood polishing (thopda) work that gives doors and furniture a rich, lasting finish.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you make doors in custom sizes?

Yes. Every door and woodwork piece is made to order in custom sizes, finishes and carving details.

Which wood do you use?

Most of our doors are made in teak wood, known for its strength, grain and long life.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Proddatur and serve Proddatur, Kadapa and the nearby villages and towns around them.

What other wood work do you make?

Cots, cupboards, TV units, dining tables, chairs, benches, choukattu (door and window frames) and wood polishing (thopda) — any wood work will be done here.

How do I order a door?

Pick a design on this website, then enquire on WhatsApp at +91 95332 35113 or call +91 99893 56819 with your measurements. We build it to your size, finish and carving details.
